Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

1. Overview

SeekReap Tier-4 ("SeekReap", "we", "our", or "Service") provides content analysis tools to help creators review their own videos for potential policy risks prior to publication. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our services.

  • TikTok Account Information: When you authenticate via TikTok Login Kit, we may receive basic profile information (such as username and user ID), subject to your authorization.
  • Video Metadata: Titles, captions, and publicly available metadata of videos you choose to analyze.
  • Content Analysis Data: Risk indicators, flags, and analysis results generated by our system.
  • Email Address: If you provide one for notifications or support.
  • Technical Information: IP address and basic usage logs for security and fraud prevention.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Authenticate users securely via TikTok
  • Provide content analysis and flagging services
  • Display analytics dashboards
  • Enable optional content publishing where authorized
  • Improve system functionality and security

We do not sell, rent, trade, or use TikTok data for advertising purposes.

4. TikTok Data Usage

Data obtained through TikTok for Developers APIs is:

  • Accessed only after explicit user authorization
  • Used solely for the functionality described in our Terms of Service
  • Processed in accordance with applicable platform policies
  • Not shared with third parties except as required by law

5. Data Retention

  • Analysis results and metadata are retained only as long as necessary to provide the service.
  • If you disconnect your TikTok account or request deletion, associated TikTok data is deleted within 30 days.
  • Security logs may be retained for up to 90 days for fraud prevention.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Disconnect your TikTok account at any time via TikTok settings
  • Request access to your stored data
  • Request deletion of your data

To submit a data request, contact us using the information below.

8. Third-Party Services

SeekReap may use secure hosting and infrastructure providers to operate the Service. These providers process data only on our behalf and under confidentiality obligations.
