Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SeekReap collects minimal data necessary for certificate generation:

  • Facebook Video IDs (for verification processing)
  • Payment details (processed by PayPal, not stored)
  • IP addresses (for fraud prevention)
  • Usage analytics (aggregate, anonymized)

2. Cookies Policy

  • Essential Cookies: Session management, navigation
  • Analytics Cookies: Usage patterns (opt-out available)
  • No Advertising Cookies: We do not sell your data

You can manage cookies via browser settings. Essential cookies cannot be disabled.

3. Data Usage

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Certificate generation and delivery
  • Payment processing (PayPal)
  • Fraud detection and prevention
  • Service improvement (anonymized analytics)

4. Data Sharing

We never share personal data with third parties except:

  • Payment processors (PayPal - PCI compliant)
  • Legal requirements (court order)

5. Data Retention

  • Video IDs: 30 days (certificate validity)
  • Payment records: 7 years (tax compliance)
  • IP logs: 90 days (fraud prevention)

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your certificates anytime
  • Request data deletion (after retention period)
  • Opt-out of analytics cookies
  • File complaints with data protection authorities
